# Zone Art Prompts

Prompts for the zone images that `ZoneId` points at but that are not yet in
`assets/images/zones/`. Each prompt is self-contained: paste it as-is.

Target format (matches the existing art, e.g. `swamp.png`):
- 512 × 512 PNG, square
- Saved to the exact path in the heading

Colours in each prompt come from the zone's `ZonePalette`, so the art and the
UI laid over it agree; the accent colour should be the brightest point of
interest in the scene.

# Zones borrowing another zone's art

These zones currently load an image made for a different zone. The paths below
are suggested new filenames: `ZoneId` still points at the borrowed image, so
change each `iconAsset` only once its new file is in `assets/images/zones/`.

Forest Mine keeps `mine.png`: the rail tunnel mouth in a mossy hillside is
already its picture. Its two siblings get their own.

Old Yew Grove and Witchlight Grove use `ZonePalette.forest`, and Maplewater
Village uses `ZonePalette.maplewater`, so their prompts stay in those colours.
Witchlight Grove's new art may justify its own palette afterwards.
